* SAS Programming: SAS (Statistical Analysis System) is a powerful software suite used for data management, analysis, and reporting. In the context of regulatory affairs, SAS programming helps analyze clinical trial data to generate tables, figures, and summaries required for regulatory submissions.

* Regulatory writing skills

* Introduction to SAS and its core functionalities (data manipulation, procedures)

* SAS programmers looking to specialize in the pharmaceutical industry

Course Providers:
